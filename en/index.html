CHOOSING A DIRECTION OF DEVELOPMENT

We can become a civilization of creators, a unified system of life on the planet.

Our knowledge and technologies give humanity an unprecedented ability to shape the future. The next stage of civilization’s development should be defined not by violence of one part of humanity against another, but by our ability to preserve life, develop human capacities and create.

KNOWLEDGE · TECHNOLOGY · CREATION
CONFLICT · LOSS · DESTRUCTION
CENTRAL VALUE

The self-identification of every person.

Human development should become one of the central tasks of civilization: developing reason, understanding one’s own “self,” and constructive abilities — the capacity to investigate, create, care and cooperate.

Human development

Education, knowledge, health, reason, consciousness and the development of human capacities.

Constructive abilities

The ability to understand, investigate, work and develop together.

Knowledge with open horizons

Open inquiry into humanity, consciousness, life, Earth and the Universe — clearly distinguishing facts, hypotheses and assumptions.

SHARED IDENTITY

A world where borders no longer make people strangers.

Countries, cultures, languages and traditions can remain unique. At the same time, we can recognize our shared identity as people of Earth. This is the next level of humanity’s self-awareness and each person’s self-identification.

I am a human of Earth.
