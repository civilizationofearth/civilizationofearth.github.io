A CIVILIZATIONAL CHOICE

Humanity has reached a point where its knowledge can either deepen division — or help create a shared future.

Our technologies can transform the world. The next stage of development can be defined not by one part of humanity triumphing over another, but by our ability to preserve life, develop human capacities and create together.

KNOWLEDGE · TECHNOLOGY · CREATION
CONFLICT · LOSS · DESTRUCTION
THE CENTRAL VALUE

The main value is the human being.

Human development should become one of the central tasks of civilization: developing reason, consciousness and constructive abilities — the capacity to understand, investigate, create, care and cooperate.

Human development

Education, thought, health and the development of human capacities.

Constructive abilities

The ability to understand, investigate, create and work together.

Knowledge without closed horizons

Open inquiry into humanity, consciousness, life, Earth and the Universe — while distinguishing facts, hypotheses and assumptions.

A SHARED IDENTITY

A world where borders no longer make people strangers.

Countries, cultures, languages and traditions can remain unique. At the same time, we can recognize an additional identity that belongs to everyone.

I am a person of Earth.

MANIFESTO

We can choose a different future.

Humanity possesses vast knowledge, technology and capabilities, yet directs a significant part of its energy toward fighting itself. The next stage of civilization can be about learning to create in partnership with one another and with the planet.
